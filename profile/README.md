Someone makes a big claim. Your mates pick a side. Truco helps find the receipts.

We’re building a native iPhone app for in-person crews and far-flung group chats: shared rounds, clear stakes, evidence-backed results, a fair challenge process, and a long memory for those “by 2030…” predictions.

### Good company. Clear rules.

- **Call it:** agree on the claim and what would prove it right.
- **Back it:** join either side and see your exact stake and possible return.
- **Settle it:** follow the evidence, challenge a shaky verdict, and give the winner their moment.

The development beta uses **pretend money only**. No deposits, card charges, cash withdrawals or redeemable balances. All the banter, without real-money payment processing.
